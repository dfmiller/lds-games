Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use the Play Web Fun home page (the “site”).

No accounts

The site has no log in and no user accounts. We do not ask you to give us your name, email address or any other details to browse it.

Information collected automatically

When you visit, certain information is collected automatically, by us and by the third-party services described below. This can include your IP address, device and browser type, language, the pages and apps you view, how long you stay, and the website or ad that referred you.

Analytics

We use analytics services to understand how the site is used, such as which apps are opened most, so we can improve it. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to recognize your browser and measure activity over time.

Advertising and cookies

The site shows advertising. Some ads promote our own apps. Others are provided by third-party advertising partners, who may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies to show ads, measure how well they perform, limit how often you see the same ad, and, where permitted, personalize ads based on your browsing activity across sites. These partners may collect and combine the information described above for those purposes, under their own privacy policies.

You can control cookies in your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them, though parts of the site may work differently if you do. You can also opt out of personalized advertising through industry tools such as optout.aboutads.info (US) or youronlinechoices.com (Europe). Where required by law, we ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies.

Fonts and hosting

The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch it. Our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs for security and operations.

Offline use

If you install the site as an app, your browser may store a copy of its files on your device so it can work offline. You can remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing site data.

Our apps

Each of our apps may handle data differently. Please check what an app says about its data before using it.

How information is used and shared

We use the information described above to run and improve the site and to show and measure advertising. We share it with the analytics and advertising providers that help us do this, and where the law requires. We do not sell your information for money.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email digitalfunmedia@gmail.com.
