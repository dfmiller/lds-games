How to play

  • You and Sim take turns flipping two cards.
  • If the two cards match, you keep the pair and go again.
  • If they don’t match, they flip back and it’s the other player’s turn.
  • Sim only knows the cards that have been turned over, and it forgets them over time.
  • Whoever collects the most pairs wins. On 5×5 and 7×7 boards the center square is empty.
  • Your wins, losses and draws are totaled on the end screen across all board sizes and saved on this device. Use Clear stats in the menu to reset them.

Clear stats?

This resets your wins, losses and draws to zero. It can’t be undone.
